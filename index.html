A tiny interactive science museum

Meet your
seven senses.

We use more than five senses to explore the world. Pick one below, try its little demo, and notice how each sense gives you different information.

07
ways to explore

👁️

Sight · noticing light and color

Your eyes help you spot shapes, color, brightness, and movement.

In daylight, colors and details are easy to see.

👂

Hearing · noticing sound

Your ears detect vibrations and help you notice pitch and rhythm.

Choose a note to hear a short tone. Turn your volume down if needed.

👃

Smell · noticing scents

Your nose picks up scent molecules and helps you recognize familiar things.

🌹

A flower can have a sweet, soft scent. This screen shows examples; it cannot produce a real smell.

👅

Taste · noticing flavors

Your taste buds help distinguish flavors, while smell adds lots of detail.

🍓

Sweet flavors are often found in ripe fruit and desserts.

✋

Touch · noticing texture

Skin senses can tell you about texture, pressure, and temperature.

Rough like tree bark

The patterns are visual examples of texture. A screen cannot recreate the feeling on your skin.

🧍

Balance · knowing which way is up

Your inner ear helps your brain sense movement and keep you upright.

🧍

Try tilting the scene, then bring it back to level. Your inner ear does this kind of sensing as you move.

🧭

Body awareness · sensing where you are

Proprioception helps you know where your body parts are without looking.

Move the dot into the target. Use the arrow buttons or your keyboard arrow keys.

A screen can show, play, and guide a demo — but smell, taste, and touch are best explored in real life.
